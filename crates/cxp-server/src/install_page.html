Use Savvagent CXP from your AI assistant

Savvagent CXP doesn't have a web page here. Add it to your AI assistant as a connector, then ask about customers, tickets and feedback in plain language. The first time, you sign in with your Savvagent account.

Connector address

{{CONNECTOR_URL}}

Claude (claude.ai or the desktop app)

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector and paste the connector address.
  3. Choose Connect, then sign in with your Savvagent account.

ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors, and turn on developer mode.
  2. Choose Create and paste the connector address.
  3. Sign in with your Savvagent account when asked.

Claude Code

  1. Run this in your terminal:
    claude mcp add --transport http savvagent-cxp {{CONNECTOR_URL}}

  2. In Claude Code, type /mcp and sign in with your Savvagent account.
